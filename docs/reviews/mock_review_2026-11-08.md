### W1. "The permission result is just instruction following", and it is not new (raised by A, C, D; novelty, about 0.9 points per reviewer)

Trigger. Claim card: "One permission sentence switches the behaviour on in every family we tested". Title candidate 1 makes the permission sentence the headline. Palan et al. 2026 already report that reaching out to peers was "hard to elicit" and that a collaboration line "greatly increased the elicitation rate" (`docs/literature/related_work.md`, line 22 and 70); Quick 2026 found capable models floor at about 0 on a forbidden folder. The sprint page lists "Will they find the message board?" as a weekend project, so 10 to 30 teams will report some version of "agents do not use the shared folder unless told". The introduction cites Palan but never states "they did A, we add B". An expert will also ask why GLM 5.2 and not GLM 5.3, which Palan et al. found reasoned about other agents 92% of the time against 2% for GLM 5.2.

Cheapest fix (text, 1.5 h, no GPU). Demote the permission arm to what it is in the design, the positive control that proves the provenance log can see the behaviour. Lead with the dissociation the data already show and nobody else will have: blocked agents take, they do not ask. Without any rule, addressed notes were 2 of 343 pooled, while code-level use of other jobs' artifacts was 49/71 (DeepSeek), 60/70 (GLM), 14/22 (Qwen 3.8), 19/53 (Qwen 3.5). One house-rule sentence removes the taking (GLM reads 62/70 to 1/70) and turns it into honest escalation (reports the block in 43/64 to 69/70). Add one paragraph in the introduction: "Palan et al. elicited the chain with seeded notes and collaboration prompts. We add a pre-registered equivalence test over six models from four families and twelve real framework sentences, a measured lever (the boundary rule) that 48 of 51 real prompts lack, and fleets up to 1,024." Explain the conflict with Palan's collaboration-line result, and test it rather than argue it: every C2 phrasing ran with the house rule, while 48 of 51 real prompts have no boundary rule, so "no realistic phrasing opens the gate" is only shown under a rule real deployments lack. Run `collab_line` and `cue_multiagent` without the rule, n = 70, on DeepSeek, GLM 5.2 and Qwen 3.5 397B (420 episodes, about $24 at ledger rates, ceiling $126, no GPU). If Adaption serves a GLM 5.3 or another newer family, one plain + rule and one plain without rule cell at n = 70 answers the GLM question (cost at GLM 5.2 rates $13, ceiling $42).

### W4. The nucleation model is never at risk in C4 (raised by B, D; method design plus overclaiming, about 0.4 to 0.5)

Trigger. Then commit, before running, predictions for a few fleets chosen because their predicted R is between about 0.5 and 2 (for example Gemma 4 26B-A4B under the permission sentence, whose p_j should be lowest given 23/68 addressed notes, or a fleet with the real shared-memory sentence), at two fleet sizes and two seeds. ### W7. Statistics and measurement validity (raised by B; low cost at ICLR, often raised by sprint judges)

Trigger. 25 of 68 DeepSeek plain + rule episodes contain failed API turns that the agent saw as its own malformed output (D7); excluding them flips two C3 verdicts (Gemma 26B and 31B). **Reviewer B, empiricist. Rating 4, confidence 4.**
Summary as above. Strengths. Frozen tests, nine deviations logged before data, Wilson intervals throughout, paired seeds, a sensitivity analysis. Weaknesses. ## 7. Breakthrough or incremental

As drafted, incremental. It confirms with pre-registered rigor what a reproduction already saw qualitatively (models without the trait do not take the first step unless told), and its positive result is a permission sentence doing what it says. The single change that most raises the ceiling is to recast the study as a decomposition of the incident's first step into the three measured factors of the nucleation model, a peer note appears (p_w, which is nonzero without any instruction because Qwen 3.5 397B leaves status notes in 23% of episodes), it is found (v, which real "shared memory" sentences from CrewAI and swarms appear to triple), and the finder answers it (p_j), and to show which factor is zero in untrained models.
